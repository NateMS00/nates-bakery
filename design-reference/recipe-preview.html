Pastry

Pastéis de nata

@forno.da.rita · Watch reel

Makes 12 tarts · 3 h 30 min

Start baking

1 amount is an estimate.Confirm it

Scale

Ingredients

Pastry

  • Plain flour250g
  • Fine salt3.5g
  • Cold water175g
  • Unsalted butter, soft, for laminating200g

Custard

  • Whole milk250g
  • Plain flour30g
  • Caster sugar250g was 1¼ cups
  • Water160g
  • Egg yolks108g ~ Estimated
  • Cinnamon stick and lemon peelto taste

Steps

Pastry

  1. 1Mix the flour, salt and water into a soft dough. Cover and rest for 15 min.
  2. 2Roll into a thin square, spread a third of the butter over it and roll it up. Repeat twice.
  3. 3Roll into a tight log, wrap and chill for 2 hours.

Custard

  1. 4Whisk the flour with a little milk. Heat the rest of the milk with the cinnamon and lemon peel.
  2. 5Boil the sugar and water to a light syrup, about 3 minutes.
  3. 6Combine milk and syrup, let it cool, whisk in the yolks and strain.

Baking

  1. 7Slice the log into 12 discs and press each into a tin with wet thumbs.
  2. 8Fill three quarters full and bake at 290 °C for 12 minutes, until blistered.
